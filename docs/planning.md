# Planning Summary (Non-Technical)

## Purpose

Explain, in simple words, what the current app is, what the next version should become, and how they differ.

## Current App (Simple Description)

Right now, the app is like a digital notebook for shop records.

It helps you:

- Keep track of who bought and who paid
- Record credit (udhar)
- See customer balances

It is strong at record keeping, but it feels more like a system than a shop experience.

## Goal (What You Want to Build)

A full shop operating system — not just records.

It should help with daily work:

- Selling
- Billing
- Credit tracking
- Supplier planning
- Knowing what to restock

## The New System (Three Simple Parts)

### 1. Customer Side

Simple and fast:

- See products
- Place order
- Pick up from shop
- See how much they owe

### 2. Shop Owner Side

Daily work made easy:

- Make bills quickly
- Mark cash or credit
- Record payments
- See who owes money

### 3. Supplier Side

Smarter purchasing:

- Know which supplier comes when
- Track what you owe them
- Plan what to order next

## Biggest Difference (In One Line)

Current app stores information.  
New app helps you take action every day.

## Why This Matters

Small shops don’t fail because of missing data.  
They fail because of missed actions:

- Forgetting who owes money
- Ordering late
- Missing stock

The new system solves those daily problems.

## Simple Explanation to Share

“Right now my app is a digital notebook for accounts.  
I’m improving it into a full system that helps run my shop daily — billing, udhar, suppliers, and reminders.”
